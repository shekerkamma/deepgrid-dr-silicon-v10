All 22 eligible guide entries have three evaluation checks, a named source download and an onward route. Home and company are intentionally absent from the guide registry because their evaluation paths are integrated in their stories. Shared `Related` navigation remains present in route bodies. Actual content-level claim validation remains the responsibility of the corrective register and named source documents; retention is not a new factual certification of old copy.

The built visual system preserves Newsreader, Inter and JetBrains Mono, the wordmark, ink, copper and bone. The hero uses the approved representative industrial assembly, with HTML socket labels and Three.js material overlays. Labels are roles, not proof of a validated board. Sensing is explicitly conceptual. The opened DG32 package and functional plate are illustrations rather than physical die-layout or measured-silicon evidence. Proposed organic-substrate integration uses an explicit proposed label and dashed frame; DG32 QFN-64 remains a different package scope.

Bone chapters organise reuse and qualification and the company route-to-market argument. Dark atlas rows keep purpose, boundary, readiness, evaluation and source adjacent. The two roadmaps are separate lanes and the four source-defined stop rules pair conditions with responses. The atlas has nine core SKU architectures plus D100; DG SDV is a separate reference platform. DG32 remains pre-silicon, and the simulated fault result is not transferred to other products.

The checked-in detector snapshot contains 24 advisories (8 colour, 16 font-size) and 2 warnings (overused-font, side-tab). It predates current workbench changes: removed literals and old line numbers cannot justify new tokens. Actual bone UI roles are now documented in DESIGN.md; actual renderer material literals are recorded separately in `.impeccable/design.json`. Industrial lighting/material shading is intentional and distinct from UI accents. Fluid hero and section type sizes serve the approved hierarchy. This documentation is not a detector rerun or a claim that warnings have been cleared.

Motion and accessibility acceptance require a readable complete fault path with reduced motion or unavailable WebGL, reliable injection/reset and pause behavior, and an illustrative motor coast after bridge disable. The source implements HTML causal labels and static raster context; concurrent motion repairs must pass implementation/runtime checks before those behaviors are called verified. Desktop/mobile layout, keyboard navigation, source links, static export and deployment similarly require the implementation owner's final checks.
